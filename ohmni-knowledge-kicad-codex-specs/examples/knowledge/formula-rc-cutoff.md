# RC Low-Pass Cutoff Frequency

## Expression

`fc = 1 / (2 * pi * R * C)`

## Variables

- `fc`: cutoff frequency in hertz
- `R`: resistance in ohms
- `C`: capacitance in farads

## Applicability

Applies to an ideal first-order passive RC low-pass filter.

## Notes

Actual behavior should be verified under the circuit's source/load conditions.
